About me!

My interests outside of science have shaped me into the researcher I am today. Read about them below!

Music

I've been playing the cello since I was eight years old, which has inspired a life full of music. I've been heavily involved in symphonies, notably the Milwaukee Youth Symphony Orchestra, and the Purdue Bands and Orchestras program. I also have experience playing the bass drum, marching tenors, and accordion.

I love theatre, and have been a part of ~10 different shows, either on-stage or in the pit orchestra.

Nature

I think it'd be pretty hard to be an environmental scientist if I didn't love nature. Luckily I do! I spend a lot of my free time hiking, visiting botanical gardens/arboretums, and I have a soft spot for macrophotography.

Other

I have some interest in weaponry, and have trained as an instinctive recurve archer. I also have involved in the sport of Dagorhir, and enjoy collecting swords.

I'm always trying some new craft--my personal favorites as of late include stained glass and chainmail.

Foreign language is another love of mine, I've had 6 years of formal Spanish instruction, and am learning Polish recreationally.
