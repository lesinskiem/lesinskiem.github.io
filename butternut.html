Butternut

I worked with the Purdue Hardwood Tree Improvement and Regeneration Center (HTIRC) for two years during my undergrad.

About

The HTIRC seeks the improvement, management, and protection of hardwoods. My specific work supported a project focused on determining the effects of hybridization of butternut with Japanese walnut on resistance to Thousand Cankers Disease.

Pathosystem

Thousand Cankers Disease (TCD) affects walnut trees (Juglans spp.). When the fungal pathogen, Geosmithia morbida, is introduced into the tree by its beetle vector, the walnut twig beetle (Pityophthorus juglandis), cankers grow and lead to tree decline.

My work!

My work for this project was largely field-based. I sampled black walnut (Juglans nigra) plantings to get material later used for genomic sequencing. Additionally, I assessed canopy dieback of butternut (Juglans cinerea) so that it could be compared with dieback of butternut-Japanese walnut hybrids (buartnut/Juglans x bixbyi). I gained my introductory experience with computational genomics with the HTIRC while I conducted population genomic analysis of the sequenced black walnut data and conducted parentage analysis of this data using genetic markers.
