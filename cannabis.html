Sassafras

This project was conducted in the summer of 2024 as part of the Research and Extension Experiences for Undergraduates (REEU) program I participated in at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville (UTK).

About

The goal of the Explore BiGG Data REEU was to "holistically prepare talented undergraduate students for graduate programs and careers in bioinformatics, genetics and genomics". As part of a research team, I worked to unravel host-pathogen interactions between Cannabis sativa and hop latent viroid.

Pathosystem

Hop latent viroid (HLVd) is a single-stranded RNA pathogen that affects Cannabis . (Juglans spp.). When the fungal pathogen, Geosmithia morbida, is introduced into the tree by its beetle vector, the walnut twig beetle (Pityophthorus juglandis), cankers grow and lead to tree decline.
